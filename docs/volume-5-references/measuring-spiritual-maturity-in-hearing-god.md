# Measuring Spiritual Maturity in Hearing God

*Measuring Spiritual Maturity in Hearing God: An Affective Domain Taxonomy for Individual and Small-Group Internalization* is the diagnostic member of the **Hearing-God Papers**.

## How MSM Is Used in the LotS Project

MSM is the full-length formalization of the measurement argument that [HFT](source-pdfs/hft-heart-formation-theology.pdf){: .pdf-popup data-pdf-label="HFT — Heart Formation Theology" } first sketched — the Affective Taxonomy mapped to the Sower — and that [MSF](source-pdfs/msf-measuring-spiritual-formation-at-scale.pdf){: .pdf-popup data-pdf-label="MSF — Measuring Spiritual Formation at Scale" } takes to scale. Its soils-to-levels mapping is the academic backbone of the heart-soil diagnostic in [V2.Exp1](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-01-heart-soil.md), and its level definitions underwrite the hearing-journey taxonomy in [V2.Exp7](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-07-hearing-with-understanding.md). Its level-specific measurement criteria inform the instruments of the [Vol 4 testing framework](../volume-4-testing-framework/section-2-testing-framework.md). MSM is the diagnostic half of a pair completed by [CSM](cultivating-spiritual-maturity-in-hearing-god.md), and the foundation on which the two applied papers — [CTG](clearing-the-ground-lower-levels.md) and [BFP](bearing-fruit-with-patience-upper-levels.md) — build.

!!! note "Integration, not replacement"

    MSM's five-level taxonomy is read across the LotS project under the integration-not-replacement framing ([V2.Exp7](../volume-2-knowing-to-doing/exploration-07-hearing-with-understanding.md#reading-these-levels-as-integration-not-replacement)): the levels name the order in which kinds of engagement first become accessible, but the mature pattern is the held integration of all five, not the replacement of earlier stages by later ones. A measured high level whose Level 1 Receiving has died is not maturity but rigidity. And a measured level gauges *formation*, never *standing* before God — fixed in Christ (Rom. 8:1) — so a low or stalled level is never a verdict on a person's acceptance (see [Vol 3, Spiritual Distance](../volume-3-quantitative-framework/exploration-04-spiritual-distance.md)).
