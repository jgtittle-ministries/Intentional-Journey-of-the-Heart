# Appendix to Exploration 5: The Four Connects, a Dated History

*Where the Four Connects came from, and how they have been run, debriefed and revised since. Drawn from my own notes and files, 2002 to 2026. No names from the archive appear here: not the men, not the churches.*

> **In one paragraph.** The Four Connects began with a question about measurement. Faith, hope and love were the basic elements I was trying to understand, and I asked how each could be measured. Testing love against its opposite showed that hate still connects you to a person. The zero point is indifference, so the real scale is connection. The two greatest commandments give love its objects: God, neighbor and self. Being connected to God means hearing and obeying him, and that leads to mission. So there were four. Since then the framework has been run, debriefed and revised in groups, retreats and workshops for more than twenty years. *Fellowship of the Heart* did not start that way of working. It is how this work has always been done.

---

## The question underneath

Two questions sit under everything that follows. The first is Jesus' word to Nicodemus: "Are you the teacher of Israel and yet you do not understand these things?" (John 3:10). He expected a teacher to understand how the Spirit works. The second was my own. Why were my prayers so ineffective? I had been taught to say, "You never know what God will do." I came to see that saying as making excuses for God.

A friend asked a third question that has stayed with me: where is the training plan for new Christians? The Four Connects, and everything built on them, are one long attempt at an answer.

## 2002: the Laws of the Spirit

I took faith, hope and love (1 Corinthians 13:13) as the elemental pieces of Scripture, worth the time to think through. For each one I asked three questions:

- **Relation.** How do they relate to each other? What does Scripture say?
- **Meaning.** What does the word really mean? This is where faith became trust: the Greek *pistis* carries both.
- **Measure.** How could you measure it? How do you measure faith, or trust? How do you measure hope?

My 2002 notes already list the essential elements, love, hope, faith and grace, beside their negatives, sin, shame and hate. They already ask what a fully functional human would look like.

## How love became connection

The measurement question was about shape. Is love like temperature, running from zero upward? Or does it run from positive through zero to negative? I tested each element against its opposite. The obvious opposite of love was hate. But holding the two in tension, I saw that hating someone actually connects you to them. The zero point is not hate but indifference: simply not caring. So the underlying scale is connection. It grew out of my trying to figure out how to measure love.

*Date:* not found in writing. It falls in the Laws of the Spirit years, before the first written use of the name in 2008.

## From connection to four

I tied connection to the two greatest commandments (Matthew 22:37–39). There, love has three objects: God, neighbor and self. And if you are connected to God, you will be listening for his word and willing to obey it. That means you are willing to find your mission and go do it. So the fourth connect is mission.

## 2002–2003: the first cycle

For about a year, three men met in a home on Saturday mornings. Each morning paired causal diagrams of Scripture with experiential prayer. The approach was tested before it was offered more widely. My onboarding note of December 2002 says I had held five or six sessions with several men "to test this approach out, to refine," and that it was ready to go to production. In January 2003 a published invitation offered it to others. It drew no one.

Twenty-three years later, a school club built on the same work also drew no one. Both times the zero was treated as data.

## 2002–2005: the parts before the name

- **About 2002.** The mission process, with its *gold mission* and *shadow mission*, came out of a week-long men's retreat. I knew its impact on me and on others, so including it was natural.
- **November 2003.** A group protocol already forms and closes a container. It also states the stance of an experiment, in my words then: "I'm documenting as we go, because I think we are breaking new ground. . . . Sometimes the doesn't work is even more informative than the does work."
- **November 2005.** A guide for starting a new group gives the build order: tell your stories, *Who Are You?*, mission clarity, heart tools, *Any Doubts?*, the hot seat, heart Bible study, and the gold and shadow mission. The parts of the Four Connects are here, but not the name.

## 2008: the name

- **July 2008.** The first written use: "Tell your story – tied to four connects." Each tool touches one connect. God: heart Bible study. Self: *Who Are You?* and clearings. Others: telling your story, and clearings. Mission: mission clarity. And a note that has governed the work since: "Tools selected to be simple tools."
- **November 2008.** The container is formed on the Four Connects. The circle opens and closes on them, and each man says "I'm in." The same protocol separates therapy from coaching. This is the ancestor of the *Fellowship of the Heart* container.
- **November 2008.** After a men's retreat, the Four Connects are the stated objective of both the retreat and the groups that follow it. Of twelve men who signed up for the follow-on sessions, seven completed half or more. Those who stayed really got it.

## 2009–2015: run, debriefed and revised

- **2009.** The first full Four Connects retreat I can find, in February, already close to the current form. Then a session on connecting with God; a three-day retreat in May with consolidated feedback and a "delivery ready" version 5 by August; and two more church retreats that autumn, one revised from version 1 to version 6. Feedback was taken after every session, often on a wall of sticky notes. More than forty recordings survive.
- **October 2009.** *The Garden of Your Heart*, the ancestor of Evening 7 of the [FotH Evening](https://fellowshipoftheheart.org/reader.html#docs%2Fevening%2Findex.md).
- **2010.** A "Four Connects 201," and a relational discipleship guidebook that names "four connections that we encourage men to make at every meeting."
- **2011–2015.** Forum days, church workshops, a nonprofit board workshop, and retreats, each with its own versions.

## 2014: the first written record

In 2014 I wrote the teaching down as letters to my children, published in pieces with the thinking visible. Each law was held at a stated level of certainty (95, 75, 50 or 25 percent), offered as a working hypothesis, and revised as the evidence came in. A law counted as known when it agreed with Scripture, came by revelation, was confirmed by others, was witnessed in the body, and bore fruit. Proof, I wrote, comes from a community of practitioners and from personal conviction. Those letters were the first mid-flight report. They grew into the volumes of *Intentional Journey of the Heart*, and the certainty levels became the three confidence tiers of the [Note on Proof and Certainty](../introduction/note-on-proof-and-certainty.md).

## Written into Intentional Journey of the Heart

[Exploration 5](exploration-05-four-connects.md) lifts the Four Connects from an organizing label to a causal sequence with directional dependencies, grounded in Matthew 22:37–39. Its tier is *Reasonably Inferred*: the sequence is confirmed by years of observation, and the mechanisms are still being worked out.

## Into Fellowship of the Heart (2024–2026)

- **The first day.** *Fellowship of the Heart* began with one full day in which all the adults walked the Four Connects end to end.
- **The prequel.** The Four Connects are now the [recommended prequel](https://fellowshipoftheheart.org/reader.html#docs%2Ffour-connects.md) to all three of its curricula, and the spine of every choice in them.
- **September 2026.** After the first evening's debrief, the Four Connects map was set to open every evening.

The story of *Fellowship of the Heart* itself is told on its own site: [How Fellowship of the Heart Got Here, 2024–2026](https://fellowshipoftheheart.org/reader.html#docs%2Fhow-we-got-here.md).

## What the history shows

- **One question.** The Four Connects were born from a question about measurement: how do you tell whether this is working? The curriculum asks the same question now. The framework and its criteria come from one question, asked twenty years apart.
- **The cycles go back.** Every retreat from 2003 on was run, debriefed and revised, with version numbers to show it.
- **The criteria have roots.** "Simple tools" (2008) is an early form of the teenager filter: *if a teenager can use it, adults can too.* Therapy set apart from coaching (2008) became the rail *therapeutic, but not therapy.* The 2005 build order became the dependency order. My 2002 guideline for leaders, that the leader is the facilitator of each man's journey and that the forming belongs to the Holy Spirit, is the leader's stance today. And the 2002 hope that new men would become co-creators, and that separate groups could start anywhere and still connect, is the invitation to run your own.
